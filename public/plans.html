How do I pay?

By Fiji QR. After you request your plan we show our payment QR code. Scan it with your banking or wallet app, pay the amount shown, and put the payment reference we give you in the note. Then send us the receipt on WhatsApp. Your plan goes on once we see the payment, usually the same day.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your badge and top spot switch off when your paid time ends. Your shop stays in the mall on Bula Free.

Can I change plans?

Yes. Request the new plan here and we'll switch it when the payment arrives.
