For businesses

Your shop, your customers, your payments. Optional paid plans support advertising and expanded storefronts.

  • Commission only. Free listing is available. Optional Featured and Premium plans are described on the Plans page. Sales commissions require separately agreed terms.
  • You keep your payments. Customers pay you directly with M-PAiSA, QR or bank transfer.
  • Monthly settlement. Any agreed sales commissions are reconciled separately. Plan payments use the instructions on the Plans page.
  • Genuine shops only. Every business gives a registration number or TIN, and we take down any listing that isn't genuine.

Privacy notice

  • We collect what you enter in this form: your name, contact details, town and, for businesses, your business details.
  • Customers: your details are seen only by the Business Hub Fiji team. They are never published or sold.
  • Businesses: if you tick "Show my business in the mall", your business name, category, what you offer, town, payments accepted, Google Maps link and phone/WhatsApp number appear publicly in the mall. Your own name, email and registration number or TIN are never shown.
  • We use your details to contact you about the hub and to check businesses are genuine. Nothing is ever sold.
  • To take your listing down at any time, message us on WhatsApp (+679 921 1153) or email us.
  • In the mall, we count taps on each shop's WhatsApp, Call and Map buttons, with no personal details attached, so we can show businesses how many customers we send them.
  • If you send us a problem report, only the Business Hub Fiji team sees it. We don't pass your name or contact details to the business.
  • To change or remove your details, email us with your registration reference (it starts with BHF-).
  • Never send passwords, bank account numbers or government ID through this form.
