Online seller listings

Policy version: 28 November 2026. This describes Business Hub Fiji’s opt-in advertising directory. It does not certify a seller’s legal compliance.

For shoppers

Each product identifies its seller and links to the seller’s store. Purchases, payment, delivery and returns are arranged with that seller. Business Hub Fiji remains responsible for its own advertising and conduct. Nothing here removes rights provided by applicable law.

Prices show the currency supplied by the seller. “From” indicates a range of variant prices. Confirm the chosen variant, availability, taxes, delivery charges and return terms before paying. Listings are refreshed when this page loads and purchase links expire after five minutes. Sponsored listings are labelled. Business Hub Fiji does not claim to be Shopify or endorsed by Shopify.

Seller permission and review

Before inclusion, an authorised seller must approve the store, specific products, categories and use of their product titles, photographs, prices and availability. The seller must confirm rights to the content. Business Hub Fiji records the permission, its date, duration and policy version, verifies store control, and reviews each product. A submitted URL alone does not grant permission or activate listings.

Only reviewed general goods are enabled in this version. Restricted, regulated, infringing or misleading offers must not be listed. Category selection alone is not evidence of a licence or approval. Listing fees or commissions require a separate agreement before any charge. No automatic commission is collected by these links.

Merchant service terms

Business Hub Fiji provides the directory and is responsible for its operation, support and handling of merchant data. Shopify is not responsible for faults or harm caused by this directory and does not provide support for it unless expressly stated by Shopify. Sellers retain ownership of their content and can withdraw permission. This policy must be presented together with the seller’s written service agreement before API access is configured.

Privacy and withdrawal

The directory reads only the approved public product fields and seller identity. It does not request Shopify customer, order or bank records. Private API credentials are kept on the server. This feature adds no advertising pixels, customer profiling or click analytics. External product images are hosted by Shopify’s CDN; loading them contacts that service. Opening a seller link contacts their store and their privacy policy applies.

To correct a listing, withdraw consent, report an intellectual-property problem, request a data copy or request deletion, contact business-hub-fiji@risingsunfj.com. Identify the seller and product URL, without sending passwords or API tokens. Operators disable withdrawn listings and remove unnecessary merchant data within 30 days, subject to applicable retention duties. Live removal requires the operator to update the approved connection configuration; it is not an automatic email process.
